# Plan consensus

This skill requires `directives-flow`. If it is not loaded, load it. It carries the cycle ([cycle.md](../directives-flow/references/cycle.md)), the roles and what to pass to each agent ([roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md)) and the recording rules. Here are only the consensus steps, from step 2 to step 8 of the cycle. The list below is numbered only for reading: the numbers cited in the text are always those of cycle.md.

You are the main session: you call each agent as `oh-my-hexflow:<role>`, one at a time, and you run what is outside its matrix.

## Before starting

- The work item is recorded and open (`work-items.<id>`), with the chosen prefix and slug. In `feat` and `fix` it came from the [interview](../interview/SKILL.md): the business side is already confirmed and the agents cover only the technical side.
- Call each `oh-my-hexflow:*` **without** `name`. With `name`, the agent runs without the `omhl-allow` matrix and without the preloaded view (its skill does not arrive), and, by the design of the `agent-role` hook, what it records must come out as `claude`.
- Each call carries the id of the work item. Whoever reads the spec also gets the **absolute** path of its folder, in the main checkout: the `dir` of `spec init`, which the planner repeats in "Spec written to". A relative path does not exist inside the worktree.
- Each call also names the language of the conversation with the person (for example "Conversation language: pt-BR"), as a fallback to the `SubagentStart` hook, never as the only channel.

## The steps

1. Call `oh-my-hexflow:analyst` with the id. It records `assumed` premises and gaps with `kind`; every business premise it suggests after the interview is late (`origin: late`, `assumed`) and only counts once the owner confirms it. In `feat` and `fix`, write in the request "business already confirmed in the interview; cover only the technical" and "stage 2 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl list --type gap --where work_item=work-items.<id>`.
2. Take to the human what is business (a `business` gap, a gap without `kind` and each late `assumed` premise) and record the confirmation with the rules of the base. The session checks with `omhl list --type premise --where work_item=work-items.<id> --where status=confirmed`.
3. Call `oh-my-hexflow:critic` only to confirm the technical premises and answer the `technical` gaps. Since there is no plan or spec folder yet, say in the request: "The absence of the folder is expected, do not ask for it and do not send the call back for that. Confirm the technical premises and answer the technical gaps of work item <id> and nothing else. The `VERDICT` of this call does not count (there is no plan to judge): fill it with whatever and list only the confirmed premises and the answered gaps." Ignore the `VERDICT` of this call: step 7 of cycle.md only counts the verdict of the rounds (steps 5 and 6 of cycle.md). The architect anchors only on a confirmed premise. Also write in the request "stage 3 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl report answers --work-item work-items.<id>`.
4. Call `oh-my-hexflow:planner` with the id, the slug and the analyst's open questions; this is the explicit request to write the spec. Write in the request "consensus mode, SHORT" ("DELIBERATE" only when the human asked or the work item is high risk). The planner does not cite in the spec a late premise that is still `assumed`; its business suggestion comes back to you, who record it as late. It creates the spec and validates it (`spec validate` is part of its work); the final check is that of step 8 of cycle.md, after the rounds. Also write in the request "stage 4 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl spec validate work-items.<id>`.
5. Call `oh-my-hexflow:architect` with the id. Pass the planner's consensus summary and ADR, and write in the request "consensus review, SHORT" (or "DELIBERATE", if step 4 used that mode). It records each `decision` with the `rests-on` to the anchor. Also write in the request "stage 5 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl list --type decision --where work_item=work-items.<id>`.
6. Call `oh-my-hexflow:critic` with the id and the spec folder. It judges the plan: for each architect `decision` it evaluates, it records a `decision` of its own, with `rests-on`, and links it to the architect's decision with a `supports` or `contradicts`. The verdict travels only in the message: do not record it. Write in the request "consensus review, SHORT" (or "DELIBERATE") and ask for the consensus summary line in the answer. It lists each recorded relation in `Records Written`, in the format of the agent itself ([critic.md](../../agents/critic.md)). Also write in the request "stage 6 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl project` (read the `## Relations` section, see [roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md)).
7. Acceptance is the verdict `ACCEPT`. `ACCEPT-WITH-RESERVATIONS` goes to the owner with the reservations, and they decide. `REJECT` or `REVISE` opens another round: send the objections back to the architect (and to the planner, if the spec changes; pass it the id and the absolute spec folder, the `dir` of `spec init`) and call the critic again. Round 1 is steps 5 and 6 of cycle.md. **There are at most 3 rounds.** On the third without acceptance, stop: show the owner the impasse (the critic's verdict and the live `contradicts`) and wait for their decision. The `contradicts` stay live and block `gate-delivery`: the owner retires them in a terminal outside Claude Code (recipe in [roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md)), or orders a review and the verifier retires them. A late premise (`origin: late`) that the owner confirms until the spec is frozen (the birth of the work item branch) reopens the plan: the architect and the critic judge again with it, the round counts in the 3 and the impasse goes to the owner; after the freeze, it enters only by `divergence` and, if the spec was wrong, by `amendment-NN`. In each round, repeat in the requests the cycle stage of the agent called (5 for the architect, 6 for the critic, 4 for the planner). The session checks with `omhl project` (read the `## Relations` section).
8. With acceptance, call `oh-my-hexflow:verifier` in an isolated context (pass only the id, the absolute spec folder and the `from`/`to` pair of each `contradicts` from earlier rounds) to check that each revised architect `decision` answers the `contradicts` and to retire the resolved `contradicts`. Copy the `Retirements` section of its message, with the `from -> to` pair of each retirement, to `<main checkout>/.omhl/notes/<id>-retiradas.md` (absolute path, in the main checkout and outside `.omhl/specs/`, so `spec-lock` does not freeze it; use `Write`, not `Bash`: the hook does not see what `Bash` writes, and `Write` is where the folder gets its `.gitignore`); step 7 of the `review` skill reads that file. The pairs are in the `contradicts: <from> -> <to>` lines of the critic's answer and in the `## Relations` section of `.omhl/projection/work-items.<id>.md` (see [roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md)). The relation points to the node id, not to the revision: without the retirement, it stays live after the revision and blocks the PR gate. A verifier with `FAIL` or `INCOMPLETE` counts as a round without acceptance: send it back to the architect (step 5 of cycle.md). Also write in the request "stage 8 of the cycle". The session checks with `omhl project` (read the `## Relations` section).
9. Only then run `omhl spec validate work-items.<id>`. The session checks the `✔ spec validate` header of that output.
10. With the spec valid, follow the end of step 8 of cycle.md: `omhl gate-worktree` and the worktree. The session checks the `reason` (`matrix-satisfied`) of the `omhl gate-worktree` that the step runs.

## Light path (`chore` and `docs`)

In `chore` and `docs` there is no spec, and the interview only runs at the owner's request. Do items 1 to 3 of the list (steps 2 and 3 of cycle.md), skip items 4 to 9 (steps 4 to 7 of cycle.md and the start of step 8) and, in item 10, run only `omhl gate-worktree`: no planner or rounds and no verifier from step 8 of cycle.md; the review of step 9 follows the `review` skill. The critic only runs the mode of item 3 (confirm the technical premises and answer the technical gaps), because there is no plan or decision to judge. The other prefixes follow the full path, with the 3 rounds.

## What to do with each agent's answer

Every answer ends with the fields **Next role:** and **Open questions for the main session:**. What to do with each one is in [roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md), in the section "What to do with the answer". In the consensus, if you disagree with **Next role:**, follow the next step of the list.

After the work item's first `decision`, the owner's answer to a business gap is recorded as a late premise (`origin: late`) and reopens the plan until the spec is frozen: the architect and the critic judge again, and the round counts in the 3 of step 7 of cycle.md; after the freeze, it enters only by `divergence` and amendment. Without the mark, the guard refuses with `origin-required`, which is not a role refusal: add `origin: late` and record again.

If an agent returns `role-record-forbidden` (or `role-revision-forbidden`, `role-answer-forbidden`, `role-retirement-forbidden`), it tried to record outside its matrix. The refusal is the answer: do not record the same content again as `claude`. Read [roles.md](../directives-flow/references/roles.md); if information is missing, record a `gap` with the suggested `kind`; if a decision is missing, take it to the human.

The `model:` line of each agent is the default; whoever calls the agent may pass another model.
