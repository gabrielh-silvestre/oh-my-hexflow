## oh-my-claudecode

- Origem: oh-my-claudecode, de Yeachan Heo.
- Versão de que o material deriva: 5.7.19.
- Licença: MIT.
- Material derivado: os sete agentes de [plugin/agents](plugin/agents): [analyst](plugin/agents/analyst.md), [planner](plugin/agents/planner.md), [architect](plugin/agents/architect.md), [critic](plugin/agents/critic.md), [code-reviewer](plugin/agents/code-reviewer.md), [security-reviewer](plugin/agents/security-reviewer.md) e [verifier](plugin/agents/verifier.md).
